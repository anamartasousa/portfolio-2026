Work from Centro

Client
Studio work at Tipo—Grafia for Turismo Centro de Portugal
Scope
Identity, book of interviews, remote-work manual
Year
2021
Role
Graphic and editorial design

The brief

Turismo Centro de Portugal commissioned a project about remote workers in Portugal in the aftermath of the pandemic.

What I did

I developed the visual identity for the project, using basic technology and dual colour screens as inspiration, and then two publications.

Outcome

I designed an icon system, a digital manual and a printed book of interviews, besides overseeing the website design following the same identity.
